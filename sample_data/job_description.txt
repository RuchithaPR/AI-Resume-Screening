help us understand customer feedback at scale.

- Experience with natural language processing and text classification
- Good knowledge of SQL and data analysis using Pandas and NumPy
- Experience with Git and Docker

Preferred / Nice to have:
- Experience with Hugging Face and LLMs
- Exposure to AWS and MLOps
- Experience with Streamlit or FastAPI
